The Game Plan Guide: Coaching Every Matchup

How game plans work in Basketball Dynasty: pick pace, offense, defense and rotation against each opponent, manage fatigue, and rest players without losing the games that matter.

Updated November 19, 2026

Play free

Most basketball GM games stop at the roster. In Basketball Dynasty you also coach. Before every game you set a game plan (pace, offense, defense and rotation) that is scored against your roster and the opponent's. The right plan wins games your talent alone would lose. The wrong one hands the other coach an edge.

The win chance is honest

The win percentage on Game Day uses exactly the same model as the game itself. It also explains itself: a line like "+5 talent · Game plan edge · On the road" tells you what is helping and what is hurting. Change the plan and the number moves before you tip off. If it drops, the matchup is telling you something.

The four choices

Pace: slow, normal or fast

Pace changes how random the game is. A fast game has more possessions and more chaos, which helps the underdog and helps athletic teams with stamina. A slow game protects a favourite and rewards teams that move the ball well. Fast pace also tires your players faster.

Offense: inside, balanced or threes

Threes pays off when you have shooters and they have weak perimeter defenders. Inside pays off when you score in the paint and they lack rim protection. Balanced is the safe default when neither edge is clear.

Defense: drop, switch or trap their star

Drop protects the rim but concedes threes, so it is a bad idea against a shooting team. Switch needs good perimeter defenders. Trap their star doubles their best player. It works against a one-man team and backfires against teams that pass well.

Rotation: tight 8, normal 9 or deep 10

A tight 8 gives your best players more minutes. That is worth a few percentage points tonight, but your starters tire quickly. A deep 10 spreads minutes and keeps legs fresh. Over a full season, the normal 9 is usually the best balance.

Read the scouting line first

Under the odds you get a one-line scouting report: the opponent's record, their best player and their identity, such as "Lives from three · weak interior defense". That is your cheat sheet. A team that lives from three makes a drop defense expensive. A team with weak interior defense is asking you to attack inside. You also see the other coach's plan, because the AI plays to its own strengths too.

How much a plan is worth

In testing across every team in the league:

  • The best plan for a matchup is worth about 3 percentage points of win chance on average over the default, and up to about 10 in the right matchup.
  • The wrong plan costs about 4 points on average.
  • There is no single best plan. The winning combination changes from opponent to opponent.

Three or four points sounds small. Over a season it is the difference between a play-in game and home court.

Fatigue and rest

Every player has fatigue. Starters build it faster than bench players, and tight rotations and fast pace build it fastest. Above about 50, a tired player plays below his rating and is more likely to get hurt. Tired players show a Tired or Exhausted badge in your starting five.

The Load management panel lists your tired players with a Rest tonight button. A rested player sits out one game and recovers much faster. Resting a star costs you that night, sometimes a lot, so pick your spots: rest him on the second game of a week against a weak opponent, not before a rivalry game.

A simple routine

  • Read the scouting line and the other coach's plan.
  • Pick offense and defense to attack their weakness and protect yours.
  • Use fast pace when you are the underdog and slow pace when you are the favourite.
  • Go tight for big games, normal the rest of the time, and deep during a heavy stretch.
  • Check Load management once a week and rest anyone who is Exhausted.

FAQ

Does the game plan affect simulated games?

Yes. Your plan stays in place for every game, including weeks you sim, until you change it.

Does the AI use game plans too?

Yes. Every opponent picks a plan that suits its roster, and Game Day shows you what it is.

Do game plans work in the playoffs?

Yes. Your plan still counts against your opponent's in every playoff game.
