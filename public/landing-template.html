Questions.

Quick answers before you start.

Is it really free?

Yes. No paywall, no premium tier, no upsell mid-game. The whole game runs in your browser.

Do I need an account?

No. Click Start new franchise and you're in. Your franchise lives in your browser's local storage.

Where are my saves? Can I lose them?

Saves live on the device you started on. From the Front Office you can Export a save file and Import it on another device, or use it as a backup before clearing your browser.

Can I play on phone and desktop?

You can play on both, but each device has its own save right now. Cross-device cloud save is on the roadmap — export/import works in the meantime.

What's the difference vs Basketball GM?

Same genre, different feel. Basketball GM is the gold standard for depth and longevity. Basketball Dynasty leans harder on stakes — scripted ownership briefings, job security, the firing mechanic, Championship Windows, a modern card-based UI. Both are free and both run in a browser.

Are the rosters real NBA players?

No — every player and team is fictional. That's deliberate: it lets us simulate firings, contract drama, and locker-room reactions without licensing constraints.

How long does a season take?

Up to you. "Play next game" runs one matchup with a starting-five decision. "Run to my pick" / "Skip to next event" sim weeks at a time. You can finish a regular season in under an hour or stretch it across multiple sessions.

What's on the roadmap?

Cross-device cloud save, online leaderboards, expanded coach hires, a deeper rivalry system, and a public changelog page.
