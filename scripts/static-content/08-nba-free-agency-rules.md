NBA free agency looks like a bidding war, but most teams can't bid at all. What a team can offer depends on where its payroll sits against the salary cap, the tax line and the two aprons. This guide explains the rules in plain English with the official 2026-27 numbers, then shows how Basketball Dynasty uses the same structure.

## The 2026-27 numbers

| Threshold | 2026-27 |
|---|---|
| Salary cap | $164.961M |
| Minimum team salary | $148.465M |
| Luxury tax line | $200.428M |

| Second apron | $221.686M |
| Non-taxpayer mid-level exception | $15.044M |

Source: the NBA's announcement of the 2026-27 salary cap.

## Cap room: the only way to bid big

A team under the cap can sign free agents up to its available room. That is the only way to sign an outside player to a big contract. A team over the cap has no room at all, whatever its record or market.

## Exceptions: what over-cap teams can offer

Over-cap teams sign outside free agents with exceptions:

- **Non-taxpayer mid-level ($15.044M):** for teams that stay below the first apron after using it. Up to four years.
- **Taxpayer mid-level ($6.064M):** for teams between the first and second apron. Up to two years.
- **Room mid-level ($9.366M):** for teams that used their cap room and still want to add a player.
- **Minimum contracts:** any team can sign players to the minimum.

Teams above the second apron can't use the mid-level exception at all. That is why expensive contenders fill out their bench with minimum contracts.

## Bird rights: keeping your own players

Bird rights let a team re-sign its own free agents for any amount up to the maximum, even if that takes it over the cap. That is how good teams stay good. A player re-signing with his own team can also get a five-year deal, one year longer than any other team can offer.

## Maximum contracts

A player's maximum salary is a share of the cap that grows with his years in the league: 25% for younger players, 30% for players with 7–9 years of service, and 35% for veterans with 10 or more.

## How Basketball Dynasty models free agency

Basketball Dynasty runs a fictional league with simplified cap numbers, but the structure is the same:

- **Under the cap:** you can offer up to your cap room.
- **Over the cap:** you can only offer the mid-level exception (once a season), the taxpayer mid-level above the tax line, the bi-annual exception, or minimum deals. Past the second apron, minimum deals only.
- **Contract lengths:** four years with cap room or the mid-level, two with smaller exceptions. Five-year deals are only for re-signing your own players.
- **Expired contracts can't be traded.** Re-sign the player first, using Bird rights.
- **Rival bids:** other teams make offers too, and only teams that can afford a player bid on him.

## FAQ

**Can a team over the cap sign a star in free agency?**
Not directly. Without cap room it can only offer an exception or the minimum, so stars usually move by sign-and-trade or by trade.

**What is the difference between the cap and the tax line?**
The cap limits how teams sign outside free agents. The tax line is where teams start paying luxury tax on payroll above it. The aprons above it add trade and signing restrictions.
